- **L3** (2026-11-27, T1 evidence from three read-only explorers; recheck any line before citing it):
  - Amounts. `orders.total_amount_cents` and `service_fee_amount_cents` are written twice by `fn_create_shipments_from_single_payment`. `orders.actual_stripe_fee_cents` is written once (`IS NULL` guard) by `stripe-webhooks`, `fn_complete_stripe_fee_reconciliation_job_leased`, `cancel-order`, and `auto-cancel-orders`. `orders.cancellation_loss_cents` accumulates (`fn_cancel_shipment`, `fn_complete_overdue_payout_refund`). `order_items` (one per shipment) holds `price_at_purchase_cents`, `commission_amount_cents`, `shipping_amount_cents` (seller-paid reserve), and `net_payout_cents`, inserted once. `shipments.shipping_cost_cents` repeats the reserve; `label_provider_cost_cents` is set by `fn_finalize_shipment_label` and can be overwritten by `fn_reconcile_shipment_label`. Seguro Selene has no column: it exists only in PaymentIntent metadata and is recomputed in refund metadata.
  - Net payout copies. `admin_connect_payout_release_view` sums `order_items.net_payout_cents`; `release-connect-payout` copies it into `connect_payout_run_shipments.net_payout_cents` and `connect_payout_runs.amount_cents`; `fn_begin_shipment_transfer` and `fn_atomic_payout_resume_mapping` check they agree.
  - Idempotency keys today: PaymentIntent `selene_pi_<key>` with UUIDv5 order and shipment ids; Transfer `<idemKey>:<runId>:<shipmentId>`; cancellation refund `cancel_shipment_<shipmentId>`; dispute refund `refund_dispute_<disputeId>`; return label PaymentIntent `return_pay_<disputeId>`; checkout recovery refund `pi_<id>_recovery`; payout run `connect_payout_runs.idempotency_key`; payout evidence append-only in `internal.connect_payout_events`.
  - Refund paths. Cancellation (buyer, seller, paid and preparing timeouts) and the overdue payout refund share `shipment_cancellation_operations` and `_shared/cancellation/shipment-cancellation.ts`. The dispute refund (`resolve-dispute-refund`) bypasses it, stores no amount, and keeps the refund id only in `disputes.admin_notes`; its basis is price plus the seller shipping reserve (#741). Checkout recovery keeps its own queue in `orders.compensation_*` and `internal.checkout_recovery_shells`. No chargeback handling exists.
  - `shipment_cancellation_operations`: primary key `shipment_id`, about 26 CHECKs, five lifecycles (claim, Stripe refund, carrier, admin review, late carrier activity). `fn_record_shipment_cancellation_refund` overwrites `refund_status` forward-only (`succeeded` can become `failed`). `label_cost_loss_cents` has three writers. `docs/specs/shipment-cancellation-coordination.md` says "do not create a second refund subsystem" and requires the shipment lock first, the `external_started_at` fence before any provider call, a frozen server-built refund request, and recovery by recorded refund, metadata lookup, or replay inside 23 hours.
  - States. `order_status` serves both `orders.status` and `shipments.status`. `trg_shipments_status` copies `internal.fn_derive_order_status` into `orders.status`; first match wins in the order dispute, refunded, cancelled, paid, preparing, shipped, delivered, all completed. Opening a dispute writes `orders.status = 'dispute'` directly and no shipment is ever `dispute`, so the next shipment change overwrites it (grep only; prove with pgTAP). `fn_resolve_dispute_to_seller` and its admin variant set `completed` with no from-status guard. Deadlines read `updated_at` (#574). No pgTAP test covers the cancelled-plus-delivered order.
  - TLC model: covers cancellation, transfer, and the overdue refund; disputes and returns are out of scope. Its README code mapping table and sync commit must follow any function rename.
  - Specs a ledger must respect: one path per shipment (platform balance, Transfer, connected balance, Payout, bank), manual payouts after completion only, payout equals the sum of included shipment nets, retry only after confirmed failure without duplicating Transfers, `paid` can become `failed`, payout evidence append-only by event identity (`docs/specs/payout-operating-model.md`, `docs/specs/connect-payout-reconciliation.md`).
- **L4** (2026-11-27, agent decisions for T2, the maintainer approves them with the document per S6):
  - D1 Shape. Two tables: `ledger_accounts` (one row per account: `kind` plus the owner it belongs to, such as a shipment or a seller) and `ledger_entries`. An entry moves `amount_cents > 0` from one account to another, so every entry balances by construction and no deferred balance trigger is needed. Entries are append-only: no UPDATE or DELETE grant, and a trigger refuses both. A wrong entry is undone by a reversal entry that points at it.
  - D2 Accounts: `buyer` (external, per order), `shipment_funds` (per shipment, money Selene holds for it), `seller_balance` (per seller, the connected account after a Transfer), `seller_bank` (external, per seller), `commission` (Selene revenue), `seguro` (Selene, pays the Stripe fees), `shipping_reserve` (per shipment), `carrier` (external, Envia), `stripe` (external, fees), `loss` (Selene losses), `return_label_fees` (Selene revenue), `unallocated_payments` (checkout recovery payments without an order).
  - D3 Idempotency. Every entry has a unique `entry_key` built from the provider or database fact it records (for example `<payment_intent>:<shipment>:commission`, `<transfer_id>`, `<refund_id>`, `<payout_id>:failed`). A replay with the same key and amount is a no-op; the same key with another amount raises.
  - D4 Writers. Only SQL functions post entries, through one internal function, in the same transaction as the state change they describe: payment capture (charge, commission, Seguro, shipping reserve per shipment), Stripe fee reconciliation, label purchase and its correction, reserve settlement at completion, Transfer completion, payout paid and failed, refund recorded as succeeded (with the reversals of commission, Seguro, and reserve, and the losses it leaves), refund failed after success, manual repayment, dispute refund with transfer reversal, return label fee, checkout recovery charge and refund. Edge Functions never post entries directly.
  - D5 Derived values. Balances come from a view over the entries. The payout amount of a shipment is the balance of its `shipment_funds` account. `order_items.net_payout_cents` and the copied amounts on payout mappings become derived or checked against the ledger; `connect_payout_runs.amount_cents` stays as the amount sent to Stripe. "What did Selene owe this seller on day X" is a sum of entries up to that day.
  - D6 Time. Each entry stores `effective_at` (when the provider says it happened) and `created_at` (when Selene recorded it).
  - D7 Growth: about 10 to 15 entries per order, 50,000 to 75,000 a month at the design target. Kept forever as financial records; #450 classifies them.
  - D8 Open questions for the maintainer: ISR and IVA withholding (#658) would add entry kinds; chargebacks (#269) are not handled today; whether the Seguro surplus or deficit is reported per month.
