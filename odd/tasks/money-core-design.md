- **L4** (2026-11-27, agent decisions for T2, the maintainer approves them with the document per S6):
  - D1 Shape. Two tables: `ledger_accounts` (one row per account: `kind` plus the owner it belongs to, such as a shipment or a seller) and `ledger_entries`. An entry moves `amount_cents > 0` from one account to another, so every entry balances by construction and no deferred balance trigger is needed. Entries are append-only: no UPDATE or DELETE grant, and a trigger refuses both. A wrong entry is undone by a reversal entry that points at it.
  - D2 Accounts: `buyer` (external, per order), `shipment_funds` (per shipment, money Selene holds for it), `seller_balance` (per seller, the connected account after a Transfer), `seller_bank` (external, per seller), `commission` (Selene revenue), `seguro` (Selene, pays the Stripe fees), `shipping_reserve` (per shipment), `carrier` (external, Envia), `stripe` (external, fees), `loss` (Selene losses), `return_label_fees` (Selene revenue), `unallocated_payments` (checkout recovery payments without an order).
  - D3 Idempotency. Every entry has a unique `entry_key` built from the provider or database fact it records (for example `<payment_intent>:<shipment>:commission`, `<transfer_id>`, `<refund_id>`, `<payout_id>:failed`). A replay with the same key and amount is a no-op; the same key with another amount raises.
  - D4 Writers. Only SQL functions post entries, through one internal function, in the same transaction as the state change they describe: payment capture (charge, commission, Seguro, shipping reserve per shipment), Stripe fee reconciliation, label purchase and its correction, reserve settlement at completion, Transfer completion, payout paid and failed, refund recorded as succeeded (with the reversals of commission, Seguro, and reserve, and the losses it leaves), refund failed after success, manual repayment, dispute refund with transfer reversal, return label fee, checkout recovery charge and refund. Edge Functions never post entries directly.
  - D5 Derived values. Balances come from a view over the entries. The payout amount of a shipment is the balance of its `shipment_funds` account. `order_items.net_payout_cents` and the copied amounts on payout mappings become derived or checked against the ledger; `connect_payout_runs.amount_cents` stays as the amount sent to Stripe. "What did Selene owe this seller on day X" is a sum of entries up to that day.
  - D6 Time. Each entry stores `effective_at` (when the provider says it happened) and `created_at` (when Selene recorded it).
  - D7 Growth: about 10 to 15 entries per order, 50,000 to 75,000 a month at the design target. Kept forever as financial records; #450 classifies them.
  - D8 Open questions for the maintainer: ISR and IVA withholding (#658) would add entry kinds; chargebacks (#269) are not handled today; whether the Seguro surplus or deficit is reported per month.
